Manual · about one afternoon

Measure the road outside your school.

Seven simple steps. You film the gate once, the tool measures every near-miss, and you get a report and a letter you can send to the road authority.

📱A phone that films
📏A tape measure
🟨8 bright markers: A4 paper, chalk or cloth
💻A laptop with Python 3.11+ and ffmpeg 7.1 or newer
🔑A Meta Model API key
1

Ask first

  • Tell the head of your school what you will film, and when.
  • Pick the busiest time: when school ends is best.
The tool blurs every tracked face and pixelates number plates in the clips, but always ask before you film.
2

Mark the road

  • With the tape measure, mark a rectangle on the road, for example 8 m long and 7 m wide.
  • Put a bright marker at each of its 4 corners. Anything works if the camera can see it clearly from above: A4 paper, a chalk mark, a bright cloth, a small cone.
  • Put 4 more markers: two lines across the road, A and B, exactly 15 m apart, one marker on each kerb. This is the check line: the tool never uses it to measure, so it can prove itself later.

📝 Open the field sheet

Print this sheet and write each number on it while you measure.

Only put markers on the road when it is safe, with someone watching the traffic.
3

Film from high up

  • Go somewhere high that looks down on the crossing: a bridge, a roof or a first-floor window.
  • Hold the phone sideways (landscape), at normal zoom. Keep it still.
  • Film for 10 to 20 minutes while people cross. All 4 corner markers must stay in view.
Do not zoom or move during the video. A steady phone gives better measurements.
4

Mark the road in the app

  • Take one picture (frame) from your video:
ffmpeg -ss 5 -i YOUR-VIDEO.mp4 -frames:v 1 frame.jpg
  • Open App, step 1. Load frame.jpg.
  • Click the 4 corner markers on the road, going round in one direction. Type the sizes you measured (8 and 7). Then click A near, B near, B far, A far, and type 15 in ‘Line A to line B’.
  • Copy the text the page makes and save it as site.json.
Want to practise first? Open App, step 1 and press Try it with our gate: it loads our frame and our 4 real corners.
5

Run it

  • Get the code and put your key in a file called .env:
git clone https://github.com/shaunmortal/crossing-watch
cd crossing-watch
echo "MODEL_API_KEY=your-key-here" > .env
python3 test_cw.py        # checks that everything works, under a minute
  • First run it for free: it only checks your video and tells you the cost.
python3 monday.py YOUR-VIDEO.mp4 site.json
  • Then the real run, with a spending limit (here, 14 dollars):
python3 monday.py YOUR-VIDEO.mp4 site.json --budget 14 --yes
About $12 for an 8-minute video. It never spends more than your --budget.
6

Answer the agent's question

  • The agent stops and asks you to measure the check line with the tape.
  • Type what the tape says. Use the R number it printed:
python3 agent.py YOUR-VIDEO.mp4 site.json --out out/YOUR-VIDEO-agent --answer R1 "15 m"
If the video and the tape agree, the agent writes the letter. Our gate: video 15.0 m, tape 15 m, 0% off.
7

Read the report and send it

  • Open out/YOUR-VIDEO/index.html: every near-miss with its blurred clip, the road map, the fix and the letter.
  • Make the printable letter, then the head of the school reads it, signs and stamps it:
python3 letter_pdf.py out/YOUR-VIDEO site.json
  • Send it with the report to the road authority. In India you can also use the government's grievance portal, CPGRAMS.
See what a finished report looks like: our gate's report.
Questions

Do I need to know coding?

No. You copy and paste the commands above.

Is it safe to share the clips?

Yes. Before it makes the clips, the tool blurs every tracked face and pixelates every number plate: SAM 3.1 finds the plates at full resolution, so cars and bikes still look like cars and bikes. A close vehicle whose plate SAM cannot find is pixelated whole. The clips keep the road's own sound, so you can hear the horn; Muse Voice Transcribe finds where anyone speaks, and those moments are silenced.

What is a near-miss?

A person and a vehicle reach the same spot within a few seconds. Under 1.5 s is serious; under 3 s is a close pass.

Can I try it without a key?

Yes: python3 pack.py sample sample/site.json makes a full report from made-up data, and the Explorer replays our real run in your browser.
