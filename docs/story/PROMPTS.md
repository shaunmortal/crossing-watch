# Story images: what to generate, and where to drop them

The landing page (`docs/index.html`) reads these files by name. A missing file just shows the chapter on a dark
gradient, so generate what you can; the page never breaks. Export each as a JPG, 1920 x 1080 (16:9), under 600 KB.
Real evidence images (blurred street, near-miss strobe, map, Muse Image's fix, the letter) are already in this folder.

Style for every prompt: cinematic photo, late afternoon golden light, an Indian college gate on a service road, dusty
asphalt, students in white shirts with backpacks, motorcycles and auto rickshaws, shallow depth of field, no text, no
logos, faces turned away or out of focus (privacy), 16:9.

## 04-phone.jpg
A student's hands holding a smartphone in landscape over a concrete highway railing, filming the road and a red
college gate far below from about 8 metres up; a yellow tape measure and white A4 sheets taped on the road are visible
in the phone's screen; golden afternoon light; the phone screen shows a red REC dot; cinematic, 16:9, no readable text.

## 07-college.jpg
Inside a college department office: a student in a white shirt shows a laptop to a senior professor in a grey jacket
sitting behind a wooden desk; on the laptop screen a top-down road map with coloured dots and a printed letter beside
it; warm lamp light, books, a small Indian flag on the desk; both faces softly out of focus; cinematic, 16:9, no text.

## 08-government.jpg
A printed letter with an official seal lying on a desk next to a laptop that shows a government web portal form, a
stamp pad and a pen; in the soft background a sandstone government building with a dome and an Indian flag; warm
light; cinematic still life, 16:9, no readable text.

## 11-after.jpg
The same college gate road, now with a raised zebra crossing and a 20 km/h sign, students crossing calmly while a
motorcycle slows before the crossing; golden afternoon light; wide shot from above, cinematic, 16:9, no readable text.
The page labels it "the crossing we are asking for; not built yet".

## 12-impact.jpg (optional)
A wide, quiet dusk shot of the same gate with the lights coming on and a few students walking home, 16:9, no text.
