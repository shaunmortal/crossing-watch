Not to scale. Near-misses happen far more often than collisions, so they can be counted now, before anyone is hurt (the traffic-safety pyramid: Laureshyn, Svensson & Hydén 2010).

Exact 95% ranges, if this recording is typical: 1.2-16.2% and 0.0-10.4% of people who crossed (Clopper-Pearson); 0.6-8.8 and 0.0-5.6 people in a recording this long (Poisson).

Where the fix goes

Why the authority can act

“In school zones and other vulnerable corridors, authorities shall systematically identify high-risk stretches using accident data, and implement context-specific measures such as raised crossings, zebra markings, traffic calming features, and deployment of trained crossing guards.”Paragraph 35.5, page 33
“We, therefore, direct the road owning agencies in the 50 cities mentioned in Annexure 46 of the Report on Road Accidents, 2023 and the NHAI to draw up an action plan and start phase-wise survey to assess the requirement of additional pedestrian crossing facilities.”Paragraph 35.6, page 34
“State Governments/Municipal Authorities/NHAI are directed to create an online grievance redressal mechanism regarding footpaths which would include complaints of encroachments on footpaths, maintenance of footpaths and also suggestions for pedestrian crossing.”Paragraph 35.11, page 37

Bhopal is one of the 50 million-plus cities in Annexure 46 of MoRTH's Road Accidents in India 2023.

Paragraph 35.5 asks authorities to identify high-risk stretches using accident data. The near-misses measured here add evidence about this crossing, alongside that accident data. Where the road authority has set up the online mechanism of paragraph 35.11, this pack can be filed there as a suggestion for a pedestrian crossing.

Source: Supreme Court of India, S. Rajaseekaran v. Union of India, Writ Petition (Civil) No. 295 of 2012, reportable order of 7 October 2025, 2025 INSC 1189 (PDF).

India's limit past a school or hospital where signs show it

“No driver shall drive at a speed exceeding twenty-five kilometre per hour or such lower speed as may be specified on the road signage while passing by a construction site or a school or a hospital, wherever indicated by signage, or on roads without footpaths and soft shoulders where pedestrians use a part of the carriageway to walk.”Regulation 16(5), PDF page 10

This rule applies where signs show it; this page reports measured speeds, not offences.

Source: Motor Vehicles (Driving) Regulations 2017, Gazette of India Extraordinary No. 525, 23 June 2017, G.S.R. 634(E) (PDF).

Where this fits in a conflict study

This pack covers steps 5-7 (baseline counts, pre-intervention conflicts, analysis) of the CDC/FIA school-zone Traffic Conflict Technique toolkit, without the trained observers step 3 normally needs. Severity here is post-encroachment time and the measured speed, a separate scale from the toolkit's observer severity scales. Step 9 comes after the fix is in place.

With 3 of the 51 people who crossed having a moving vehicle through the same spot within 3 s (3 distinct vehicles), the pre-registered after-fix test (G7 in GATES.md, one-sided Fisher exact test at 5%) calls for 2 dismissals filmed after the fix, from the same spot for as long each.

How this was measured

SAM 3.1 tracked the pedestrians and vehicles it found, at most 16 objects per video request (a note under the counts says when a request was full). Four points on the road turned pixels into metres. Muse Spark looked at the person in each near-miss, most dangerous first, and removed tracks that were not people; a card says when it did not look. A near-miss is a pedestrian and a moving vehicle on the same spot (within 1.0 m) less than 1.5 s apart (post-encroachment time); 1.5-3.0 s is reported as a close pass. Death risk: Rosén & Sander (2009), Accident Analysis & Prevention 41(3):536-542, for adults hit by a car front; children are not covered by that study. Speed-limit rows replay the same moment with the vehicle no faster than the limit, a 1.5 s reaction and 7.0 m/s² braking. Taped 8 m x 7 m rectangle (F1-F2 on the wall-side edge, N1-N2 on the gate-side edge), clicked on the stabilized frame at 300 s (F2 on the frame at 990 s, after a parked car left it). Camera 8.3 m above the road, hand-held; stabilize.py locks every frame to the 300 s frame.
