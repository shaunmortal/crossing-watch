How do you know distances on the road?

1. Video frame

Click four points on the road surface, going round an area in one direction. With a tape, use the corners of the rectangle you measured. With a map, use four points you can also find on the map, spread over the crossing: zebra corners, kerb corners, lane-line ends.

Open a frame to begin.

Advanced options

Take a full frame of the video itself, not a screenshot with player bars: ffmpeg -ss S -i VIDEO -frames:v 1 frame.jpg, with S the start of the site's window_s in seconds (0 without one). The page scales it to the 640 px analysis size.

Optional, for the speed check (gate G6): after the four rectangle corners, click the four speed-line tape marks, line A near kerb, line B near kerb, line B far kerb, line A far kerb, and give the taped distance between the lines. The page writes trap from them, and check (line A to line B along the near kerb, which the case agent asks a person to tape). SITE.json is empty at 5 to 7 points.
