Step 1 · works here, no key

Mark the road: pixels become metres.

What happensMark the 4 corners of a measured rectangle on the road with anything bright the camera can see clearly: A4 paper, chalk, a bright cloth. Film it, load one frame, click the 4 markers, type the sizes. The tool turns the picture into a road map in metres, so speeds and gaps can be measured.
Download our gate's frameOpen the tool full screen ↗

Faces in the sample frame are blurred. The tool runs in your browser; nothing is uploaded. Best on a laptop.

Step 2 · needs a Meta Model API key

Run it: Meta's models do the seeing.

What happensSAM 3.1 tracks every person and vehicle; code measures who reached the same spot how many seconds apart; Muse Spark looks at each person and removes riders and things that are not people. Below are lines from our real run's log, in order (long lines cut where marked ...).
Replay · our run log, 2 Nov 2026

The funnel75 raw near-miss candidates → Muse Spark checks each person → 5 kept → 2 serious and 3 close passes. The two highlighted lines are 2 of the ones Muse Spark removed: a person who was riding the bike, and something that was not a person.

About $12 of SAM for an 8.6-minute video at Meta's list prices. Python 3.11+ and ffmpeg; code on GitHub.

Step 3 · the case agent, on Muse Spark

The agent checks itself: it asks for a tape measure.

What happensBefore it trusts a single metre, the agent stops and asks a person to measure a second line with a tape, one the maths never used. If the video and the tape agree, the letter goes ahead; then it waits for the head of the school. This is the real log.
